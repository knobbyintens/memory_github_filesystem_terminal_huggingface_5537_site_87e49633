# Ilse Vandermeer

Fourth-year PhD candidate at the Ardenne Institute for Speech and Language (AISL)

## Research focus

computational morphology and low-resource language technology

## Research interests

- morphological segmentation
- dependency parsing for endangered languages
- code-switched child speech
- historical dialect corpora

## Education

- PhD in Language Technology (2022-2026) at Delft-Haven University
- MSc in Computational Linguistics (2020-2022) at Delft-Haven University
- BSc in Linguistics (2017-2020) at Port Ellery College

## Research experience

- Research intern at the European Language Resources Archive (2021-2022)
- Visiting researcher at the Karelia Field Linguistics Unit (2023)

## Award

- Aurora Foundation Doctoral Mobility Award 2025

## First-author publications

- Graph Rewriting for Morphological Segmentation of Polysynthetic Languages (2025). Proceedings of the 19th Workshop on Computational Morphology (WCM 2025) https://doi.org/10.9900/wcm.2025.09.
- Bootstrapping Dependency Parsing for Low-Resource Karelian Varieties (2024). Proceedings of the 7th Conference on Low-Resource Language Technologies (LRLT 2024) https://doi.org/10.9900/lrlt.2024.14.

## Co-authored publications

- Evaluating Multilingual Speech Models on Code-Switched Child Speech (2025). Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Multilingual Speech Processing (CMSP 2025) https://doi.org/10.9900/cmsp.2025.21.
- A Digital Corpus of Historical Finnic Dialect Transcriptions (2023). Journal of Digital Heritage Linguistics 4(1).

## Skills

Python, Cython, PyTorch, spaCy, ELAN, LaTeX
